# Community conduct

Be precise, respectful, and curious. Critique protocols, code, threat models, and claims rather than people. Provide reproducible examples where possible. Do not publish private evidence, credentials, personal information, or uncoordinated vulnerability details.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and deceptive claims are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict repeated abuse.

Project discussion should remain focused on open cryptographic verification and evidence interoperability.
