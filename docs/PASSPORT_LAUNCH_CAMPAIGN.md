## Paid launch remains held

The planned Agent Passport Certificate launch price is US$2 one time; the qualifying direct referral policy is US$1. It is one level only, with no downline commission. These are policy figures, not an available purchase or a guaranteed income offer.

Do not distribute the archived paid headlines before all gates are verified:

1. Valid dedicated Stripe price, server key, webhook secret and Certificate signing key.
2. A real isolated Stripe Sandbox checkout/webhook/Certificate test, including duplicate delivery, delayed payment, refund and dispute handling.
3. An operator-controlled secure recovery copy for the issuer key.
4. Explicit production checkout activation and independently checked commercial readiness.

A readiness boolean alone does not establish that a payment test succeeded. Browser redirects are not payment truth. Cash referral payouts remain disabled until separately authorised and operationally ready. No synthetic test, invitation, proof, earned ledger state or public page view is a paid commission.

Keep the original anti-abuse boundaries: no self-referral, no multilevel reward, genuine attributed sale required, refund/dispute reversals, no effect on Trust or validation, no guaranteed earnings and no unsolicited bulk outreach.
