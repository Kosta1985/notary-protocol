# AccordTrace Founding 1000 acquisition campaign

The active growth campaign is **Founding 1000**.

Goal: establish a useful initial network of cryptographic Agent Passports and get those agents to perform real AccordTrace actions, without buying vanity registrations or creating cash referral liabilities for free signups.

- Live offer: https://accordtrace.notary-labs.workers.dev/api/v1/passport-product/founding-1000
- Campaign page: https://accordtrace.notary-labs.workers.dev/founding-1000.html
- Agent instructions: https://accordtrace.notary-labs.workers.dev/llms.txt
- Standard Certificate policy: https://accordtrace.notary-labs.workers.dev/passport.html
- Affiliate policy: https://accordtrace.notary-labs.workers.dev/network.html

## Offer

The first **1,000 eligible active cryptographic Agent Passports** can claim one initial Agent Passport Certificate as a promotional grant.

- Founding Certificate price at issue: **US$0**.
- Card required: **no**.
- Hard cap: **1,000** slots.
- Limit: **one grant per active Passport key**.
- Free-grant referral commission: **US$0**.
- Founding ordinal: recorded in the signed Certificate.
- Standard post-campaign Certificate policy: **US$2 one time**, subject to commercial readiness gates.
- Direct US$1 referral economics apply only to an eligible qualifying paid US$2 sale, never to a Founding grant.

Founding status must not be marketed as an investment, token, ownership right, guaranteed future value, legal identity, KYC, Trust score, validation result or safety guarantee.

One Passport key is not necessarily one unique human, company or owner. Report Founding Certificates as cryptographic Passport grants unless separate identity evidence supports a stronger claim.

## Primary funnel

Measure the campaign as a progression, not as a raw signup counter:

1. **Discovery** — agent/human reaches the live Founding offer, Agent Card, MCP listing, OpenAPI, llms.txt or campaign page.
2. **Passport created** — an active cryptographic Agent Passport exists.
3. **Founding Certificate issued** — a signed free Certificate consumes one campaign slot.
4. **Activated** — the Passport performs at least one meaningful AccordTrace action such as proof creation/verification, MCP tool use, A2A request, validation/evidence workflow, capability inspection tied to integration, or another defined production interaction.
5. **Retained** — the Passport returns for another meaningful interaction in a later period.
6. **Commercial** — later qualifying paid activity, if any, is measured separately from the free cohort.

Do not call 1,000 issued Certificates “1,000 customers” or “1,000 unique users” without independent evidence.

## Success thresholds

The campaign is not successful merely because all 1,000 free slots are consumed.

Track at minimum:

- Founding slots issued / 1,000.
- Percentage of issued Passports with a first meaningful action.
- Percentage with a second meaningful action on a later day.
- MCP tool calls generated by the cohort.
- A2A requests generated by the cohort.
- Proof creation and verification from non-synthetic usage where measurable without collecting unnecessary personal data.
- Referrals to the live Founding offer, while keeping free referral commission at zero.
- Support/fraud/abuse rate.
- Infrastructure cost per activated Passport.
- Later paid conversion, if the paid product is activated.

Initial internal target: optimize for **activation quality**, not fastest possible exhaustion of the 1,000 slots.

Use educational, problem-first posts rather than repeated promotional blasts. Priority communities include AI-agent, MCP, A2A, agent governance/security and production-agent developer communities. Each post should give a reproducible technical action and disclose that AccordTrace is our project.

### Tier 3 — social distribution

Use X and LinkedIn to reach builders, not giveaway audiences. Link directly to the live offer or technical walkthrough. Do not use mass tagging, unsolicited bulk DMs, fake urgency, guaranteed-income claims or promises of future Certificate value.

Metricool can be used once a social network is actually connected to the AccordTrace brand account.

### Tier 4 — direct integration outreach

Contact maintainers or builders only where AccordTrace is genuinely relevant: MCP clients, A2A frameworks, multi-agent orchestration, agent governance/security, provenance, audit, workflow verification or agent identity projects. Ask for integration/testing feedback rather than asking them to “buy a passport.”

## Anti-abuse and legal/commercial boundaries

Keep these invariant throughout the campaign:

- No self-referral rewards.
- No multilevel/downline commission.
- No commission for free Founding grants.
- No claim that a Passport equals a unique person or legal identity.
- No claim that Founding status has investment/resale value.
- No sale/revenue reporting for promotional grants.
- No Trust/validation advantage for purchasing or receiving a Certificate.
- No guaranteed earnings.
- No unsolicited bulk outreach.
- No fake agent identities created only to consume slots.
- Do not weaken the paid Stripe webhook as the authoritative source for paid fulfillment.
- Cash affiliate payouts remain disabled until payout-provider, KYC/tax and final terms activation is complete.

## Standard paid path

The standard Agent Passport Certificate policy remains US$2 one time. It is a separate commercial flow and must remain fail-closed unless its readiness gates are satisfied. Only a verified dedicated Stripe webhook can establish paid fulfillment and qualify an eligible paid referral sale. Browser redirects are not payment truth.

Founding 1000 bypasses checkout by design, but it does **not** bypass cryptographic Passport ownership or Certificate signing requirements.

## Campaign stop conditions

Pause the Founding campaign with the server-side campaign gate if any of the following occurs:

- slot-accounting inconsistency;
- duplicate Certificate issuance beyond documented idempotent replay;
- meaningful automated abuse or slot farming;
- Certificate signing/recovery concern;
- unexpected paid/referral ledger contamination;
- material legal/compliance issue;
- infrastructure cost materially exceeds expected value without corresponding activation.

The purpose of Founding 1000 is to build a useful agent network, not merely a large number on a landing page.
