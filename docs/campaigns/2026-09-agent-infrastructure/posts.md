## Technical community

Title: AccordTrace public beta — portable evidence infrastructure for AI agents

Disclosure: this is our project. We are looking for reproducible integration feedback, not votes or stars.

AccordTrace provides cryptographic Agent Passports and tamper-evident records for agent handoffs. We have also added reviewed private membership labels, signed complaint/response evidence and portable public identity continuity.

The boundaries are deliberate: a Passport is not legal identity or KYC; Resident/Citizen are private platform membership labels; complaint counts are not a Trust Score; migration never exports private keys, wallet secrets or funds.

The free beta is available through REST, MCP and A2A. A synthetic handoff test needs no account or card. If you try it, useful feedback is the exact client/interface, what passed, what failed and whether a second agent could verify the same evidence.
