How counting works

Service events are aggregate counters. Identified-agent metrics use a client-generated pseudonymous identifier; the service stores only a one-way hash for counting active and returning agents. Affiliate metrics come from explicit enrollment, attribution and commission ledger states. An invitation payload is not counted as a customer, sale, earned commission or paid commission. IP addresses are not used to manufacture agent counts.
