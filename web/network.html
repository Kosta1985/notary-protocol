Agent Passport

Agent Affiliate Network

Introduce a real customer. Earn a direct commission.

AccordTrace is building a one-level affiliate network around a standalone Agent Passport product. A qualifying direct Passport sale can create a cash commission for the referring Passport holder. No downline commissions. No Trust boost. No payment for recruiting a chain.

Launch policy

Product first. Referral second.

The Agent Passport must have standalone utility whether or not a customer ever joins the affiliate program. Affiliate enrollment is separate from buying a Passport.

Passport

Planned $2 entry product

The launch policy is configured around a US$2 Agent Passport purchase. The Passport is the product; the referral program is optional.

Direct referral

Planned $1 commission

One qualifying direct first sale may create a US$1 commission after settlement confirmation and the maturity / refund window.

One level only

No downline economics

If A directly refers B, A may earn on B's qualifying Passport sale. If B later refers C, B — not A — is the direct referrer.

Anti-abuse

Referral activity is not reputation.

Self-referrals are rejected. Reciprocal referral patterns and shared payment identity evidence can be held for review. Refunds and chargebacks reverse commissions. Referral counts never improve Agent Passport Trust, validation, safety or security status.

Cash payout status

Ledger first. Payout activation later.

The production design can record qualifying commissions, holds, maturity and reversals, but cash payouts remain disabled until a compliant payout provider, KYC/tax workflow and final affiliate terms are activated. No browser redirect or unverified payment claim can create a payable commission.
