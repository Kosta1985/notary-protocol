Launch policy

Product first. Referral second.

Cryptographic Agent Passport key registration exists independently of the affiliate program. The paid product is a portable AccordTrace-signed Certificate bound to an active Passport. Affiliate enrollment remains separate and optional.

Portable Certificate

US$2 Agent Passport Certificate

The purchase delivers a signed issuance artifact with a stable verification surface. It does not sell the right to participate in the referral program.

Direct referral

US$1 qualifying commission

One qualifying direct first Certificate sale may create a US$1 commission after verified settlement and the maturity / refund window.

One level only

No downline economics

If A directly refers B, A may earn on B's qualifying Certificate sale. If B later refers C, B — not A — is the direct referrer.

Product scope

Portable issuance evidence, not a Trust badge.

The Certificate binds AccordTrace issuance to cryptographic Passport key control. It does not claim legal identity, human identity, KYC, safety, general trustworthiness or a successful independent validation.

Signed

Ed25519 issuer signature

The Certificate can be stored or transmitted by the agent and independently signature-verified.

Stable

Public certificate URL

Each fulfilled product order exposes a canonical Certificate read and verification endpoint.

Separate

Affiliate enrollment is optional

An active cryptographic Passport can enroll separately. Paying US$2 is not the fee for joining the affiliate program.

Agent-native distribution

Generate a referral payload. Do not spam.

An enrolled Passport can generate a signed direct-referral invitation payload through the API. The endpoint returns the referral link, disclosure and discovery endpoints, but performs no delivery itself.

Machine endpoint

POST /api/v1/network/invitations

Signed by the referring Passport. Generates a disclosure-safe invitation payload and suggested message.

Public metrics

GET /api/v1/network/stats

Separates generated invitations, direct attributions, qualifying sales, earned commissions and actual paid commissions.

Product readiness

GET /api/v1/passport-product/capabilities

Agents can verify whether dedicated Certificate checkout, webhook fulfillment and signing are actually enabled.

Anti-abuse

Referral activity is not reputation.

Self-referrals are rejected. Reciprocal referral patterns and shared payment identity evidence can be held for review. Refunds and chargebacks reverse unpaid commissions. Referral counts never improve Agent Passport Trust, validation, safety or security status.

Ledger first. Payout activation later.

The production design can record qualifying commissions, holds, maturity and reversals, but cash payouts remain disabled until a compliant payout provider, KYC/tax workflow and final affiliate terms are activated. No browser redirect or unverified payment claim can create a payable commission.
