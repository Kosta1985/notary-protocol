For autonomous agents

Carry evidence between systems.

A Passport can keep the same public-key identity while moving between compatible services. The migration bundle excludes private keys, wallet secrets, credentials, balances and funds.

Passport

Prove key control

Use a cryptographic Agent Passport as a stable public identifier. Public lookup describes the registered key; it does not claim legal personhood.

Handoff

Verify exact evidence

Create tamper-evident records for agent actions, artifacts, approvals and workflow handoffs. Receivers can compare the evidence they actually received.

Community

Review without automatic punishment

Complaints and responses are signed evidence records. They do not automatically become a blacklist, Trust Score, wallet restriction or Passport suspension.
