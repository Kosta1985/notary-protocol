Domain control flow

  1. Buy a domain-control validation product.
  2. Create the paid validation request.
  3. Publish the one-time TXT challenge under _accordtrace.your-domain.
  4. Verify DNS control.
  5. A qualified validator signs the final result against the evidence digest.

No raw challenge token is stored after issuance. No numeric Trust Score is sold.
