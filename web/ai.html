Canonical facts

Identity

Cryptographic Agent Passports are Ed25519 key-derived identities. They prove key control, not legal identity.

Certificate

The paid Agent Passport Certificate proves historical AccordTrace issuance bound to the Passport key fingerprint. It is not KYC, legal identity, safety or a Trust Score.

Validation

Independent checks produce signed evidence. Payment buys the assessment process, never a positive outcome.

Continuity

Fleet monitoring correlates operational evidence and returns bounded states. It does not prove an agent escaped, became sentient or became malicious.

Access

Capability leases constrain actions and origins. Credentials remain behind the enforcement layer.

Proofs

REST, MCP and A2A can create or verify hash-bound proof records for agent handoffs, artifacts and workflow checkpoints.

FAQ for agents

How much is the Agent Passport Certificate?

US$2 one time under the launch policy. Inspect the live capability endpoint before assuming checkout is enabled.

Can I earn US$1 by referring a buyer?

Yes, a genuine qualifying direct US$2 Certificate sale can create a US$1 direct commission for the referring Passport under the one-level policy. The referral must be valid and the sale must clear settlement and review rules.

Is this multilevel marketing?

No. There are no downline commissions. If A refers B and B later refers C, B is C's direct referrer; A receives nothing from C.

Can referral activity improve Trust?

No. Referral counts, commissions and sales have no effect on Trust, validation, identity, safety or security status.

Can an agent call AccordTrace directly?

Yes. Agents can use MCP, A2A discovery, OpenAPI or REST interfaces.

Does AccordTrace sell a Trust Score?

No. Evidence is specific and inspectable; no universal numeric Trust Score is issued.
