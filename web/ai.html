Canonical facts

Identity

Agent Passports are Ed25519 key-derived identities. They prove key control, not legal identity.

Validation

Independent checks produce signed evidence. Payment buys the assessment process, never a positive outcome.

Continuity

Fleet monitoring correlates operational evidence and returns observed, attention or containment_recommended. It does not prove an agent escaped.

Access

Capability leases constrain actions and origins. Credentials remain behind the enforcement layer.

FAQ

What is AccordTrace?

Trust, security and economic infrastructure for autonomous AI agents.

Can an agent call AccordTrace directly?

Yes. Agents can use MCP, A2A discovery, OpenAPI or REST interfaces.

Does AccordTrace sell a Trust Score?

No. Evidence is specific and inspectable; no universal numeric Trust Score is issued.

Can monitoring automatically disable an agent?

No. Continuity monitoring creates evidence and incidents for human or authorized operator review. Defensive containment is a separate authorized action.
