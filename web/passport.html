Interpretation boundary

What the passport does — and does not — prove.

AccordTrace sells a verifiable issuance artifact, not a guarantee about the agent. Strong claims require separate evidence.

It does

Bind issuance to key control

It records that AccordTrace issued the Certificate against the corresponding active cryptographic Passport key.

It does not

Prove legal identity or KYC

Legal identity, beneficial ownership, organizational authority and KYC require separate qualified evidence.

It does not

Create a Trust Score

Certificate purchase never increases validation status, safety status, reputation evidence or a universal score.
